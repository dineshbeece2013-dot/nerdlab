Docker: Running It, and Reaching It

Two separate questions

Building an image is one skill. Running one and getting a request into it is another, and it is where most first days with Docker are actually spent.

A container is deliberately sealed off. It has its own network, its own view of the world, and by default nothing on your machine can reach inside it. That isolation is the feature — it is also why a perfectly healthy container can look completely broken.

This lab is three of those moments, and the commands that tell you which one you are in: docker ps, docker ps -a, and docker logs.

Your turn
