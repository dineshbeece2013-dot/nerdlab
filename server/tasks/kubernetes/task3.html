Kubernetes: Changing Version Without Going Down

The point of running several copies

Three copies of an app is not only about handling load. It is what makes it possible to replace the app while people are using it: take one copy out, upgrade it, put it back, repeat. The other two carry the traffic in the meantime.

Kubernetes calls this a rolling update, and it is the default. Two settings control how brave it is. maxUnavailable is how many copies may be missing at once. maxSurge is how many extra it may start before removing an old one.

There is also a Recreate strategy, which stops everything and then starts the new version. It is simpler, faster, and takes the site down for the duration — occasionally that is the right choice, and you should know when you are making it.
